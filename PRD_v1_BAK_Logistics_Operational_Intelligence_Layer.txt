1. Document Purpose

This PRD defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the BAK Logistics Operational Intelligence Layer pilot. It serves as the single source of truth for engineering, QA, and stakeholder alignment during the 6-week deployment cycle.


2. Product Vision

Build a non-disruptive digital overlay that digitizes yard capacity management and automates pre-departure compliance validation for BAK Logistics. The system transforms invisible operational friction into measurable, controllable data - creating an immutable audit trail that shields BAK from statutory fines, reputational damage, and internal security vulnerabilities.


3. Strategic Context

3.1 Business Drivers
- Zimbabwe Government "Zero Tolerance" axle-load enforcement campaign (Nov 2025)
- September 2025 BAK security incident exposing manual gate vulnerabilities
- Landlocked Zimbabwe faces 75% higher transport costs than coastal economies
- Industry evidence: AI-driven yard orchestration delivers 50%+ throughput improvements
- Ryder System benchmark: 85% gate processing reduction, 5.7x 3-year ROI

3.2 Success Criteria
- Gate processing time reduction measurable within 30 days of go-live
- Zero compliance violations on loads processed through the system
- Complete digital audit trail for every gate release
- Adoption rate >80% among targeted shift supervisors


4. Target Users & Personas

4.1 Primary Users

Persona: Dispatch Supervisor (Gate)
- Role: Manages vehicle entry, queue assignment, dock allocation
- Pain Points: Fragmented communication, no visibility into yard state, paper-based checks
- Goals: Rapid gate processing, accurate dock assignment, zero compliance escapes
- Devices: Ruggedized mobile tablet (primary), desktop dashboard (secondary)

Persona: Facility Manager
- Role: Oversees yard operations, reports to executive leadership
- Pain Points: No operational baselines, reactive problem solving, manual reporting
- Goals: Real-time visibility, performance analytics, exception alerts
- Devices: Desktop dashboard, mobile phone for alerts

Persona: Operations Supervisor
- Role: Handles compliance exceptions, load rebalancing, escalation decisions
- Pain Points: Delayed notification of failures, no quarantine workflow, paper trails
- Goals: Immediate exception alerts, clear escalation paths, defensible audit records
- Devices: Mobile phone, desktop dashboard

4.2 Secondary Users
- Executive Leadership: Weekly/monthly performance reports
- Compliance Officer: Audit log review, regulatory dispute evidence
- IT Administrator: User management, RBAC configuration, system health monitoring


5. Functional Requirements

5.1 Module A: Capacity Command Centre

FR-A1: Live Queue Management
- The system shall display a real-time list of all vehicles currently in the yard
- Each vehicle entry shall capture: license plate, driver name, entry timestamp, cargo type, expected destination, assigned dock (if any)
- The system shall calculate and display wait time per vehicle (current time minus entry timestamp)
- The system shall allow Dispatch Supervisors to assign vehicles to specific docks via drag-and-drop or selection interface
- The system shall enforce dock capacity limits (configurable per facility)
- The system shall highlight vehicles exceeding configurable wait-time thresholds (default: 60 minutes)
- The system shall support vehicle status states: QUEUED, ASSIGNED, LOADING, COMPLETED, QUARANTINED

FR-A2: Asset Status Dashboards
- The system shall display real-time availability of handling equipment: forklifts, trailers, pallet jacks
- Each asset shall have a status: AVAILABLE, IN_USE, MAINTENANCE, OFF_DUTY
- The system shall allow Dispatch Supervisors to assign available equipment to specific docks/vehicles
- The system shall log all asset assignments with timestamp and supervisor ID
- The system shall alert when asset utilization exceeds 85% of available fleet (configurable)

FR-A3: Performance Analytics
- The system shall automatically calculate average turnaround time per vehicle (entry to exit)
- The system shall calculate average wait time per vehicle (entry to dock assignment)
- The system shall calculate dock utilization percentage (time dock is occupied vs. available)
- The system shall calculate asset utilization percentage (time asset is in use vs. available)
- The system shall generate daily, weekly, and monthly reports exportable to CSV and PDF
- The system shall identify peak-hour bottlenecks through time-series analysis
- The system shall provide a baseline establishment report after 14 days of operation

5.2 Module B: Smart Compliance Gatekeeper

FR-B1: Digital Clearances
- The system shall present a tablet-optimized pre-departure checklist for each vehicle
- The checklist shall include: driver license verification, vehicle registration, cargo manifest, weight certificate, axle load calculation
- The system shall support photo capture for cargo condition documentation
- The system shall require digital signature/tap-to-confirm from the Dispatch Supervisor
- The system shall timestamp every checklist completion with supervisor ID and geolocation (if available)
- The system shall prevent gate release until all mandatory checklist items are completed

FR-B2: Rule-Based Load Validation
- The system shall allow configurable axle load limits per vehicle type and route
- The system shall calculate total cargo weight against vehicle gross vehicle mass (GVM) rating
- The system shall flag loads exceeding configured thresholds before gate release
- The system shall display a clear PASS/FAIL status with specific violation details
- The system shall support override by Operations Supervisor with mandatory reason capture and secondary approval
- The system shall maintain a 99%+ accuracy target on automated calculations

FR-B3: Immutable Audit Trail
- The system shall log every system interaction: login, data entry, status change, gate release, exception override
- Each log entry shall include: timestamp, user ID, action type, before/after state, device identifier
- The system shall prevent log modification or deletion by any user role
- The system shall support log export in tamper-evident format (e.g., signed JSON)
- The system shall retain logs for minimum 7 years to meet statutory requirements
- The system shall provide rapid search and filtering by date range, user, vehicle, or action type

5.3 Cross-Cutting Requirements

FR-C1: User Authentication & RBAC
- The system shall support email/password and PIN-based authentication for tablet users
- The system shall enforce role-based access: DISPATCH_SUPERVISOR, FACILITY_MANAGER, OPERATIONS_SUPERVISOR, EXECUTIVE, ADMIN, COMPLIANCE_OFFICER
- Each role shall have predefined permissions (see Appendix A: RBAC Matrix)
- The system shall enforce session timeout after 15 minutes of inactivity (configurable)
- The system shall support multi-factor authentication for ADMIN and EXECUTIVE roles

FR-C2: Alerting & Notifications
- The system shall send real-time alerts for: compliance failure, quarantine trigger, excessive wait time, equipment shortage
- Alerts shall be delivered via in-app notification, SMS, and email (configurable per user)
- The system shall support escalation chains (e.g., if Operations Supervisor does not respond within 10 minutes, alert Facility Manager)
- The system shall maintain an alert history log

FR-C3: Offline Resilience
- The system shall support offline data capture on tablets with local storage
- The system shall queue offline actions and sync automatically upon connectivity restoration
- The system shall display clear offline/online status indicators
- The system shall resolve sync conflicts using timestamp-based last-write-wins with manual override capability


6. Non-Functional Requirements

6.1 Performance
- Page load time: <2 seconds for all primary interfaces on 3G equivalent connectivity
- Gate processing workflow: <30 seconds from vehicle selection to compliance result
- Real-time dashboard refresh: <5 seconds latency for status updates
- Report generation: <10 seconds for daily reports, <60 seconds for monthly reports
- Concurrent users: Support minimum 50 simultaneous users per facility

6.2 Reliability & Availability
- System uptime target: 99.5% during operational hours (06:00-22:00)
- Scheduled maintenance windows: Maximum 2 hours weekly, announced 48 hours in advance
- Data backup: Automated daily backups with 30-day retention
- Disaster recovery: RPO <1 hour, RTO <4 hours

6.3 Security
- Data in transit: TLS 1.3 encryption minimum
- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption
- Password storage: bcrypt hashing with salt
- API authentication: JWT tokens with 24-hour expiry
- Audit log integrity: Cryptographic hashing chain (each log entry hashes previous entry hash)
- Penetration testing: Annual third-party assessment

6.4 Scalability
- Architecture shall support horizontal scaling to additional BAK facilities without code changes
- Database design shall support multi-tenant facility isolation
- API rate limiting: 1000 requests/minute per facility

6.5 Usability
- Tablet interface optimized for 10-inch screens with touch targets minimum 48x48dp
- Color-blind accessible status indicators (not relying solely on red/green)
- Support for English language interface (Shona and Ndebele localization in Phase 2)
- On-screen contextual help for all compliance checklist items

6.6 Compliance
- Data protection: Adhere to Zimbabwe Data Protection Act principles
- Audit standards: Support Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) and Transport Ministry inspection requirements
- Record retention: 7-year minimum for all compliance and gate release records


7. User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

7.1 US-001: Vehicle Entry Registration
As a Dispatch Supervisor, I want to register a vehicle entering the yard so that it appears in the live queue.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given I am on the Gate Entry screen, when I enter license plate, driver name, cargo type, and expected destination, then the vehicle appears in the Live Queue with status QUEUED and timestamp
- Given I omit the license plate field, when I attempt to submit, then the system displays a validation error and prevents submission
- Given I enter a duplicate license plate for a vehicle already in QUEUED status, then the system warns me and requires confirmation

7.2 US-002: Dock Assignment
As a Dispatch Supervisor, I want to assign a queued vehicle to an available dock so that loading can commence.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given a vehicle in QUEUED status, when I select an AVAILABLE dock, then the vehicle status changes to ASSIGNED and the dock status changes to OCCUPIED
- Given I attempt to assign a vehicle to an already OCCUPIED dock, then the system prevents assignment and shows current occupant
- Given a vehicle has been in QUEUED status for >60 minutes, when I view the queue, then the vehicle is highlighted in amber

7.3 US-003: Pre-Departure Compliance Check
As a Dispatch Supervisor, I want to complete a digital pre-departure checklist so that the vehicle can be cleared for gate release.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given a vehicle in LOADING or COMPLETED status, when I open the Compliance Check screen, then I see all mandatory checklist items
- Given I complete all checklist items and all automated validations pass, when I submit, then the system displays PASS status and enables gate release
- Given an axle load calculation exceeds the configured limit, when I submit, then the system displays FAIL status, prevents gate release, and triggers quarantine workflow
- Given I am offline, when I complete the checklist, then the system stores data locally and syncs when connectivity returns

7.4 US-004: Compliance Exception Override
As an Operations Supervisor, I want to override a failed compliance check with documented justification so that legitimate exceptions can proceed.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given a vehicle in QUARANTINED status due to compliance failure, when I review the failure details, then I can initiate an override request
- Given I initiate an override, when I enter a mandatory reason (minimum 20 characters) and request secondary approval, then the vehicle status changes to PENDING_OVERRIDE
- Given a second Operations Supervisor approves the override, when they confirm, then the vehicle status changes to OVERRIDE_APPROVED and gate release is enabled
- Given an override is executed, when I view the audit trail, then the override reason, requester, and approver are all logged immutably

7.5 US-005: Real-Time Yard Visibility
As a Facility Manager, I want to view a dashboard showing all current yard activity so that I can identify bottlenecks proactively.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given I am on the Facility Dashboard, when I load the page, then I see: total vehicles in yard, average wait time, dock utilization percentage, equipment availability, and active alerts
- Given a vehicle exceeds the wait time threshold, when the threshold is crossed, then an alert appears on the dashboard within 5 seconds
- Given I select a date range, when I request a performance report, then the system generates a downloadable PDF/CSV within 10 seconds

7.6 US-006: Audit Trail Review
As a Compliance Officer, I want to search and export audit logs so that I can provide evidence during regulatory inspections.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Given I am on the Audit Log screen, when I enter search criteria (date range, user, vehicle, action type), then the system displays matching results within 3 seconds
- Given I select log entries, when I choose export, then the system generates a tamper-evident JSON file with cryptographic signatures
- Given I attempt to modify or delete a log entry, when I try, then the system denies the action and logs the attempt


8. Data Requirements

8.1 Core Entities
- Facility: ID, name, location, timezone, operating hours, dock count, equipment fleet size
- Vehicle: ID, license plate, type, GVM rating, axle configuration, owner/operator
- Driver: ID, name, license number, license expiry, contact number, photo
- Dock: ID, facility ID, name, capacity, status, current assignment
- Equipment: ID, facility ID, type, status, current assignment, maintenance schedule
- QueueEntry: ID, vehicle ID, facility ID, entry timestamp, exit timestamp, status, assigned dock, assigned supervisor
- ComplianceCheck: ID, queueEntry ID, checklist items, results, pass/fail status, supervisor ID, timestamp, override record
- AuditLog: ID, timestamp, user ID, action, entity type, entity ID, before state, after state, device ID, session ID, hash chain
- Alert: ID, type, severity, triggered timestamp, acknowledged timestamp, acknowledged by, escalation level

8.2 Data Retention
- Active operational data: 2 years online
- Historical operational data: 5 years in warm storage
- Audit logs: 7 years in cold storage with cryptographic integrity
- Alert history: 2 years


9. Integration Requirements

9.1 Phase 1 (Pilot - Standalone)
- No integration with existing WMS/ERP required
- Manual data entry for vehicle, driver, and cargo information
- Standalone user authentication (no SSO)

9.2 Phase 2 (Post-Pilot - API Integration)
- RESTful/GraphQL API for WMS/ERP synchronization
- Webhook support for real-time event propagation
- Potential integrations: RFID gate readers, ANPR cameras, digital weighbridge interfaces
- SSO via SAML 2.0 or OAuth 2.0

12. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Low tablet adoption among supervisors | Medium | High | Champion Program with peer-to-peer support; simplified UI; on-site training |
| Unreliable internet connectivity at facility | High | Medium | Robust offline mode with local storage and auto-sync |
| Resistance to change from manual processes | Medium | High | Executive sponsorship; clear ROI communication; gradual rollout |
| Compliance parameter misconfiguration | Low | High | Validation workshop with BAK compliance team; test environment verification |
| Data accuracy issues from manual entry | Medium | Medium | Input validation rules; duplicate detection; mandatory field enforcement |
| Scope creep beyond pilot boundaries | Medium | Medium | Strict change control process; documented Phase 2 backlog |
| Hardware failure (tablets) | Low | Medium | Spare device provisioning; rapid replacement SLA |
